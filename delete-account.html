Delete Your Account

Gastronomy — Kuwait Gastronomy & Culinary Arts (KGCA)

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

If you still have the app installed, this is the fastest way:

  1. Open Gastronomy and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your current password and confirm.

Your account and its data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, send us a deletion request from the email address registered to your account.

Email a deletion request

You can also reach us on WhatsApp (+965 9974 3229).

Please include your registered email address and, if you know it, your membership ID (for example KGCA-X00000). We verify ownership before deleting anything. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Name, last name, email addressDeleted
Password / login credentialsDeleted
Phone number, nationality, Civil IDDeleted
Sector, workplace, positionDeleted
Membership plan, status and member QR codeDeleted
Event bookings and registrationsDeleted
Payment and invoice recordsRetained for the period required by Kuwaiti law to meet accounting and tax obligations, then deleted

We never sell your data, and we do not keep a copy of your account after deletion beyond what is listed above.

Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Any remaining time on a paid membership is forfeited and is not refunded.
  • Your event bookings are cancelled.
  • If you have an active recurring subscription, cancel it first.

حذف الحساب

من داخل التطبيق: افتح تطبيق Gastronomy وسجّل الدخول، ثم انتقل إلى الملف الشخصي واضغط حذف الحساب، وأدخل كلمة المرور للتأكيد.

إذا حذفت التطبيق: أرسل طلب حذف من البريد الإلكتروني المسجّل في حسابك إلى info@kuwaitgastronomy.com أو عبر واتساب، مع ذكر بريدك الإلكتروني ورقم العضوية إن وُجد. يتم تنفيذ الطلب خلال ٣٠ يوماً.

يتم حذف الاسم والبريد الإلكتروني وكلمة المرور ورقم الهاتف والجنسية والرقم المدني وبيانات العمل والعضوية ورمز QR والحجوزات. أما سجلات الدفع فتُحفظ للمدة المطلوبة نظاماً لأغراض محاسبية وضريبية ثم تُحذف. الحذف نهائي ولا يمكن التراجع عنه.
